About Me

Bioprocess & Data Scientist

Bioprocess and Data Scientist with 5+ years of experience across GMP- and GxP-regulated biopharma, biotech manufacturing, and quality control, holding an MSc in Data Science. I transform complex process data into contextualized, analysis-ready data through robust metadata management, taxonomies, ontologies, and standardized multi-vendor pipelines across upstream operations.

A passionate and agile scientist driven by innovation, digital transformation, and data governance best practices. Specializing in data stewardship and bridging modern bioprocessing with data automation to drive process quality and development.

Core Skills & Expertise

  • Data Architecture & Governance: Metadata management, taxonomies, ontologies, data stewardship, and data governance frameworks.
  • Data Engineering & Analytics: Automated multi-vendor data pipelines, Python, Power BI, and transformation into contextualized, analysis-ready data.
  • Bioprocess Development: Upstream and downstream processing, bioreactor scale-up, sensor integration, and digital manufacturing platforms.
  • Regulatory & Quality Compliance: GMP, GxP, GDP, GLP, strict adherence to SOPs, and full audit / inspection readiness.
  • Professional Agility: Cross-functional collaboration, technical training, rapid adaptation to dynamic environments, and critical problem-solving.
