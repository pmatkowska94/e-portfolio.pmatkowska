Developed Predictive ANN Modelling Framework for Scale-Up

Master of Science Research Project

Scale-up remains one of the major challenges in modern biomanufacturing of therapeutic products. There is currently no standardised, data-driven framework to support efficient scale-up of cell-based processes, as existing datasets are typically small and research remains costly and resource-intensive. This study therefore aimed to develop a data pipeline for a predictive model capable of transferring knowledge across multi-vendor bioprocessing systems.
